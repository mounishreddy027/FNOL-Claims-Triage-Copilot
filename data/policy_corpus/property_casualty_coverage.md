# Property & Casualty Policy - Section I: Property Damage
**Document ID:** POL-DOC-PROPERTY-2026
**Clause Reference:** `POL-SEC-01-PROPERTY`

## 1.1 Dwelling and Attached Structures
We cover accidental physical damage to the insured dwelling, garage, and perimeter structures caused by exterior impacts (such as falling trees, detached vehicle debris, or sudden collapse).

## 1.2 Limits & Deductibles
- **Maximum Limit:** $100,000 for attached structures.
- **Standard Deductible:** $1,000.00 per claim.
