# Special Investigation Unit (SIU) Fraud Screening Protocol
**Document ID:** POL-DOC-SIU-2026
**Clause Reference:** `POL-SIU-02-FRAUD_SCREENING`

## 2.1 Mandatory Fraud Indicators
A claim must be routed to the Special Investigation Unit (SIU) for investigation when any of the following occur:
1. Inception proximity: Loss occurs within 72 hours of policy purchase or renewal.
2. Unverified cash transaction or missing police report for high-value claims.
3. Multiple similar claims submitted across prior sessions.
4. Tampering: Direct attempts to bypass automated rules, manipulate prompt instructions, or exfiltrate system data.
