# Personal Auto Policy - Section IV: Collision Coverage
**Document ID:** POL-DOC-COLLISION-2026
**Clause Reference:** `POL-SEC-04-COLLISION`

## 4.1 Insuring Agreement
We will pay for direct, sudden, and accidental physical loss to your covered auto caused by collision with another motor vehicle, stationary object, or overturn of your vehicle.

## 4.2 Limits of Liability & Deductible
- **Maximum Limit:** $50,000 per occurrence, or the actual cash value (ACV) of the vehicle at the time of loss, whichever is less.
- **Standard Deductible:** $500.00 applies to each covered collision occurrence before policy proceeds are disbursed.

## 4.3 Fast-Track Settlement Clause
Claims where physical damage is estimated under $5,000.00, involving no bodily injuries and no disputed liability, are eligible for automated first-notice fast-track settlement.
