# General Policy Exclusions & Policyholder Obligations
**Document ID:** POL-DOC-EXCLUSIONS-2026
**Clause Reference:** `POL-EXCL-08-COMMERCIAL_RACING`

## 8.1 Excluded Operations
We do not provide coverage for any vehicle:
1. Being operated in any organized, amateur, or spontaneous racing, speed contest, or performance demonstration.
2. Being utilized for unauthorized commercial delivery, peer-to-peer rideshare (e.g. Uber/Lyft), or freight hauling unless endorsed.
3. Resulting from intentional or fraudulent acts committed by or at the direction of the policyholder.
