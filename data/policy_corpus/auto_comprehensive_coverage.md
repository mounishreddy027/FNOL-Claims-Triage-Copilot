# Personal Auto Policy - Section VI: Comprehensive Coverage
**Document ID:** POL-DOC-COMPREHENSIVE-2026
**Clause Reference:** `POL-SEC-06-COMPREHENSIVE`

## 6.1 Insuring Agreement
We will pay for direct, sudden, and accidental physical loss to your covered auto resulting from perils other than collision.

## 6.2 Covered Perils
Covered causes of loss include:
1. Theft, larceny, or unrecovered stolen vehicle.
2. Fire, explosion, or earthquake.
3. Windstorm, hail, water, or flood damage.
4. Vandalism, malicious mischief, or civil commotion.
5. Impact with a bird or domestic/wild animal.
6. Breakage of window glass without vehicular impact.

## 6.3 Limits of Liability & Deductible
- **Maximum Limit:** $45,000 per occurrence.
- **Standard Deductible:** $250.00 per occurrence.
