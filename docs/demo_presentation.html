1. Executive Summary & The Problem We Solve

In Property & Casualty insurance, First Notice of Loss (FNOL) is the initial intake moment where policyholders report an accident. Traditional intake is slow, frustrating, and expensive:

  • High Loss Adjustment Expenses (LAE): Manual intake costs $45 to $75 per claim in desk adjuster hours and call center overhead.
  • Painful Cycle Times: It takes 3 to 5 business days simply to parse a narrative, verify policy coverage, screen fraud, and route the file to an adjuster queue.
  • Customer Churn: 15% of insureds switch carriers following a delayed, opaque claims intake experience.
  • Fraud Leakage: Insurance fraud costs the U.S. property-casualty industry over $308 Billion annually.
The Copilot Value Proposition:
An intelligent, multi-agent AI copilot that ingests messy loss narratives, masks all confidential claimant PII, retrieves exact contractual policy clauses, evaluates fraud anomaly scores via FastMCP, and assigns queues in sub-second time (872 ms) for $0.0002 per claim, freeing desk adjusters to focus on high-severity casualty claims.

4. Enterprise Governance: Fail-Closed Architecture

Why Insurance Leadership Can Trust This Copilot:
Unlike naive LLM wrappers, the FNOL Copilot enforces Fail-Closed Governance. If any microservice fails, database disconnects, or policy clause is missing, the system never defaults to approval. It immediately appends an error code, disables auto-approval, and routes to a human claims specialist.
